**VOCData.nl** is an overview of useful digital tools and datasets related to the research of the Dutch East India Company (VOC) archives. It is independent, non-official, and non-comprehensive. It is, by and large, a collection of datasets, tools and website I have bookmarked over the years. The focus was on qualitatively solid, open access data. Data behind paywalls or with overly restrictive licenses has generally been ignored.

### Maintenance of the tools and datasets
Most tools on this site rely on open-access datasets. The tools are linked "as-is" for the community to explore and use and the data and tools are maintained by their creators, if at all.
