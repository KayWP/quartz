This project is a digital recreation of the library of the Convent of San Pablo in Manila. This library was since scattered to the winds, but these documents are now digitally reunited. Although not directly about the Dutch East India Company, there is plenty of overlap. It serves as an entry-point into Spanish Colonialism in the Philippines. Some texts in the archive are supported with critical analysis from scholars.
