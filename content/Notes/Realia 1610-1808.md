A digitized version of the 47500 entries the VOC made in their *realia* in order to help their colleagues navigate the 211000 pages of resolutions of the meetings of the High Council in Batavia. The dataset creators have categorized the 47500 entries into 2050 subject topics.

Unfortunately, the dataset creators are not made clear. The data is based on an OCR'd version of physical publications from the _Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen_ created in 1882-1886.
