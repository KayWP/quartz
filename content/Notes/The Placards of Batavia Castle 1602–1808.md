A collection of ordinances or laws issued by Batavia and the government of Ceylon. Initially published as printed sources (*Neerlandsch Indisch Plakaatboek* and *Ceylonees Plakaatboek*), the dataset is essentially the running text of the books, with added metadata about the governor(-general) who issued it, and date of issue.

Note that it is unclear how comprehensive the original books were.

Additionally, the data manager or creator is unclear for this dataset.
