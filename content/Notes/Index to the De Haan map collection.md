An index of the maps in the *De Haan* collection of maps, including descriptions, scale, units of measurement, topic, location in the archive, the material the map is made of and more. These maps used to be part of the VOC archives, but were turned into a seperate collection by Van der Chijs and De Haan.

Unfortunately, it is unclear who managed the data in the digitization process.
