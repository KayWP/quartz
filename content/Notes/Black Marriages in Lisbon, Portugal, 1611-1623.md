A dataset that does not directly connect to the Dutch East India Company. Nonetheless, as a complementary dataset on Portuguese slavery and African mobility it can provde context for VOC activities in the overlapping Portuguese-Dutch imperial world, particularly in Africa and the Indian Ocean. Be aware that the CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 license is quite restrictive for reuse.
