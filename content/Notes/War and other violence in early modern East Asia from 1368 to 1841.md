This is an extensive dataset of over 1,400 entries that measures war and other violence in early modern East Asia from 1368 to 1841, showing conflicts, participants and outcomes. Do be aware of the bias in the sources used to compile the list, which are primarily Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese.
