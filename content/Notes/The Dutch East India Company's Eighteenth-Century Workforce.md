This is a dataset on 460,452 individuals employed by the Dutch East India Company (Verenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie, VOC) in the seventeenth and especially the eighteenth centuries, developed from 774,200 muster records in the ‘VOC-opvarenden’ collection.
