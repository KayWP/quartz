A comprehensive database of all 8,000+ voyages of the VOC between 1595 and 1795. It provides standardized data on ship names, their tonnage, the chamber (Kamer) responsible, departure/arrival dates, and the names of the masters. Since the tool was launched, the data has been enriched and published as [[Dutch-Asiatic Shipping (DAS) – Enriched Dataset]].
