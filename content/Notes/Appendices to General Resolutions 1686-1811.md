"The Appendices to the Resolutions of Batavia Castle are miscellaneous documents received by the General Secretariat of the Governor-General and the Council of Dutch Asia in Batavia Castle." This dataset provides an index of document titles, the creator of the documents, the vessels and people named, document type, and the location of the documents in the archive.
