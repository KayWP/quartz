An experimental Word2Vec model trained on GLOBALISE transcriptions. Enables discovery of spelling variants, synonyms, and contextual relationships in early modern VOC texts. It allows users to search for words that are used in a 'similar context' as the current one. It was used to create [[voc-gloss-explorer|the VOC gloss-explorer]] and is overall an useful tool to get to know your search terms better.
