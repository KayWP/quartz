This dataset focuses on the VOC (Dutch East India Company), drawing from 18th-century Naamboekjes—booklets detailing employment data for VOC personnel. It provides insights into roles, appointments, and geographic assignments, aiming to enhance accessibility to VOC archives via structured data on diverse personnel.
