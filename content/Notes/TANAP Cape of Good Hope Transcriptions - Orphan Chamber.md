This archive covers the inventories of deceased people created by the Orphan Chamber of the Cape of Good Hope from 1673-1844. It consists of the transcriptions of the TEPC-project (Transcription of Estate Papers at the Cape of Good Hope).

The people annotated in this dataset were later extracted into the [[Overview of People in the TEPC Inventories (1673-1844)]] dataset.
