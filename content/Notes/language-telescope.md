HTR Hub Language Telescope visualizes semantically connected terms as star-systems, letting you explore words that are 'close' to each other as used in historical text. As such it is similar to the [[globalise-word2vec-lab|GLOBALISE Word2Vec tool]], albeit with a nice interface. It is not fully clear what texts the telescope was created from.
