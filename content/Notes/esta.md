A curated database reconstructing historical slave trade across the Indian Ocean and Maritime Asia. Tracks over 5,300 voyages, covering contexts from legalized trade to private human trafficking. It allows the user to explore numerous datasets, including [[ESTA Database Locations]], [[Mozambique Slave Traders (1750–1897)]], [[Monsoon Traders – 18th Century Makassar Shipping Data]] & [[French & Dutch Slave Trade on Madagascar (1641–1810)]].
