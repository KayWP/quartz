This dataset includes all translated incoming and outgoing diplomatic letters included in the Daily Journals of Batavia Castle and the series of Incoming and Outgoing Missives. Additionally, it has indexes on rulers and places.

Take note that the authors of the dataset are not clearly indicated, making proper attribution difficult.

> [!warning] CC BY-NC-SA and scientific datasets

For scientific datasets, the **NC (NonCommercial)** clause can be problematic because it may prevent reuse by commercial researchers, industry, or organisations whose activities include commercial purposes.
