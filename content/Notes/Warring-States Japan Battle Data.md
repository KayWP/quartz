This dataset covers 2,889 battles occurring within Japan during its warring-states period, from 1467 to 1600. The dataset contains fifteen variables covering various features of the battles, including the date, location, participants, initiators, and victors, among others. Although the timespan strictly predates the Dutch East India Company, the locations and other data are still relevant into the seventeenth century.
