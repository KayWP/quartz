The contract books, kept in Batavia, contained copies of the various treaties and contracts the Dutch East India Company made with other parties in their area of operations. Each of those contracts is published in printed source publications. This collection of three datasets contains short descriptions of all the contracts, and an index on places and people mentioned or relevant.

For modern perspectives on these treaties, be sure to look at the [Historical Treaties of Southeast Asia](https://sea-treaties.org/) project.
