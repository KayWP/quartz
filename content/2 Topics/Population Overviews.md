Historical population records offer a fragmented but valuable view of communities across the Dutch colonial world.
## Datasets

### Population & census records
These datasets over overviews of inhabitants, but not exact numbers, even if the original source often did.
- [[VOC Census Records of Cochin (1700-1775)|VOC Census Records of Cochin (1700–1775)]] — Census records documenting the population of Cochin under VOC rule across the eighteenth century.
- [Vrijburgers Kaap de Goede Hoop 1716](Vrijburgers%20Kaap%20de%20Goede%20Hoop%201716) — Data on the free settler population at the Cape of Good Hope in 1716.
- [[Overview of People in the TEPC Inventories (1673-1844)|Overview of People in the TEPC Inventories (1673–1844)]] — An overview of people recorded in inventories, providing evidence for individuals and social relationships at the Cape.

### Demographical overviews
This dataset includes exact numbers of inhabitants.
- [[Enslaved Demography Batavia (1678–1807)]] — Demographic data on the enslaved population of Batavia, including patterns of origin, age, sex, and population change.

## Related sources

Some resources are not primarily population datasets, but can nevertheless provide useful evidence for studying people, communities, and demographic patterns.
- [[Namebooks of the Dutch East India Company (1730–1794)]] — Registers of names and individuals recorded by the VOC, offering additional evidence for population history and patterns of mobility.
